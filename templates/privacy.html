Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data Collection & Use

We respect your privacy. {{ bot_name }} stores only minimal data required to provide its services, specifically:

  • Your Telegram User ID and subscription status.
  • Your configured preferences (e.g. custom captions, thumbnails, destination chat ID).

User sessions are encrypted on our servers using authenticated AES-GCM encryption. We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

3. Data Deletion and Session Termination

You have full control over your session. Sending the /logout command instantly terminates the active Telegram session and removes your session credentials from the database.
